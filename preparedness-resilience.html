Our expertise

Preparedness & Resilience

Preparedness is not measured by the quality of a plan. It is measured by an institution’s ability to act when conditions change.

The challenge

Why this matters

Many organizations possess plans, protocols and technical capacities but remain uncertain about whether systems will work under pressure. Roles may be unclear, assumptions untested and readiness uneven across teams, locations or partners.

How we work

A structured path from complexity to action.

Every engagement is tailored to the institution, operating environment and decision at hand.

  1. Assess institutional readiness, capacities and critical dependencies
  2. Develop realistic scenarios based on priority risks
  3. Test plans, roles and coordination through simulations
  4. Identify bottlenecks and translate lessons into corrective actions
  5. Strengthen anticipatory action, monitoring and organizational resilience

Practical advisory support built around client needs.

Institutional preparedness assessments

Readiness reviews

Contingency planning
