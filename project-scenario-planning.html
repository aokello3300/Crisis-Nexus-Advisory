Strategic Scenario Planning

Prepare leaders for plausible futures before they become urgent realities.

Engagement overview

Focused on practical institutional value.

A leadership process that identifies critical uncertainties, develops plausible scenarios and stress-tests strategy, assumptions and preparedness.

Typical components

  • Horizon scanning
  • Critical uncertainty analysis
  • Scenario construction
  • Strategy stress-testing
  • Decision triggers and signposts
The result

A clearer path from diagnosis to decision and action.

Shared understanding

Leadership teams align around the context, risk and core decision.

Practical choices

Options and trade-offs are made visible, structured and defensible.

Implementation pathway

Recommendations translate into roles, triggers, milestones and accountability.
