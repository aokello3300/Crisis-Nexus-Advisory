Our methodology

The Crisis Decision Architecture™

A practical framework for moving from fragmented information and uncertainty to informed judgement and effective implementation.

01 — Understand the environmentAnalyze the political, economic, security, climate and operational context to identify the developments that matter most.
02 — Assess risk and opportunityExamine how risks interact and identify emerging opportunities, decision points and plausible scenarios.
03 — Develop strategic optionsEvaluate alternatives through feasibility, resources, assumptions, stakeholder implications and expected outcomes.
04 — Support implementationTranslate decisions into triggers, governance arrangements, responsibilities, monitoring and practical roadmaps.
The difference

Less information overload. More decision value.

The framework is designed to focus leadership attention on what matters, clarify choices and ensure that recommendations can be implemented.

  • From information to insight
  • From uncertainty to informed judgement
  • From reactive response to proactive preparedness
  • From fragmented thinking to integrated decision-making
  • From strategy to action

Better understanding is essential. But value is only created when understanding leads to action.
