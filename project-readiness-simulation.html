Preparedness & Resilience

Emergency Readiness & Simulation Programme

A practical readiness programme that tests plans, systems, leadership and coordination under pressure.

The challenge

Leaders need more than analysis. They need a clear path from insight to decision and implementation.

This engagement is tailored to the institution’s mandate, operating environment and decision context. It combines evidence, stakeholder insight, structured facilitation and practical implementation design.

Typical deliverables

  • Scenario design
  • simulation exercise
  • observer framework
  • after-action review
  • improvement plan

How the engagement works

A practical four-stage process.

01

Diagnose

Clarify the decision, context, stakeholders, constraints and evidence base.

02

Analyse

Assess risks, opportunities, options, assumptions and consequences.

03

Decide

Facilitate leadership alignment around choices, triggers and accountabilities.

04

Implement

Translate the decision into a realistic roadmap, monitoring and adaptation.
